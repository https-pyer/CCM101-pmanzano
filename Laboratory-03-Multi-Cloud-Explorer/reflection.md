During this laboratory mission, I learned more about the three major cloud platforms: **Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. ☁️ Among the three, **GCP impressed me the most** because of its strong services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Kubernetes, data management, and application development. 🤖 I also learned that AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide important cloud services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, security, and container or Kubernetes services. 💻 They also use a **pay-as-you-go** model, allowing users to pay based on the resources they use. 💰 However, choosing the right cloud platform depends on the **business requirements** of an organization. 🏢 Factors such as budget, performance, security, scalability, existing technologies, and future goals should be considered before making a decision. 📊 For example, a startup may focus on affordable services, while a large company may prioritize scalability and reliability. A company that already uses Microsoft products may also find Azure more suitable.
