# 🎯 Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

During this laboratory mission, I gained a deeper understanding of the three major cloud platforms: **Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. ☁️ Among the three, **GCP impressed me the most** because of its strong capabilities in **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Kubernetes, data management, and application development**. 🤖 I also learned that AWS, Azure, and GCP share many similarities, as they all provide essential services such as **virtual machines, cloud storage, databases, networking, security, and container services**. 💻 They also follow a **pay-as-you-go pricing model**, allowing organizations to pay based on their actual resource usage. 💰

However, selecting the right cloud platform depends greatly on an organization's **business requirements, budget, performance needs, security, scalability, existing technologies, and future goals**. 📊 For example, startups may prioritize affordable and flexible services, while large organizations may focus more on scalability, reliability, and global availability. Companies already using Microsoft technologies may also benefit from choosing Azure.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me that there is no single cloud provider that is the best for every organization. 🧑‍💻 I learned that it is important to understand the client's requirements first, compare available services, and recommend a solution that best matches their needs. This experience also improved my **decision-making, problem-solving, research, and communication skills**. 🧠

Finally, my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** became more organized and informative after completing this mission. 📚 I added cloud research, comparison tables, decision matrices, Linux investigation results, and client recommendations. Overall, this mission strengthened my knowledge of cloud computing and helped me develop a more practical approach to **evaluating, selecting, and recommending cloud solutions based on real-world business requirements**. 🚀
